# Dashboard Overview

## Overview

This dashboard is designed to provide a clear and professional snapshot of sales performance across the business. It brings together revenue, quantity, regional distribution, and product-line contribution in a single interactive view.

## Core Reporting Areas

### 1. Revenue and Quantity Overview
The top section of the report highlights headline KPIs such as total sales, total quantity, and year-to-date sales. These metrics give decision-makers a quick sense of business momentum.

### 2. Product Performance
Product line analysis helps identify which categories contribute most to revenue. This supports inventory planning, campaign targeting, and strategic prioritization.

### 3. Regional Sales Comparison
Country-level analysis highlights where demand is strongest. This can guide resource allocation, sales expansion, and regional promotional strategy.

### 4. Time-Based Trends
Monthly performance analysis captures seasonality and trend direction. It enables teams to compare current momentum against prior periods and spot anomalies early.

## Strategic Purpose

The dashboard is valuable for:

- leadership reporting
- weekly or monthly business reviews
- sales performance evaluation
- identifying underperforming regions or products
- making data-informed commercial decisions

## Target Audience

- Sales Managers
- Business Analysts
- Operations Teams
- Executive Leadership
- Strategy and Planning Stakeholders
